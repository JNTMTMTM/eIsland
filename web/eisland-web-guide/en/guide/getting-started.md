# eIsland Getting Started

This article introduces the main usage of eIsland.

## Main Operations

eIsland primarily uses **mouse scroll wheel** operations. Scrolling at designated positions is more convenient than clicking.

To switch between interfaces, keep your mouse **close to the top of eIsland**, then scroll the mouse wheel to navigate between pages.
