# Synced Lyrics

This article introduces the lyrics display feature of eIsland.

## Platform Support

### High-Precision Matching

- QQ Music
- Qishui Music
- NetEase Cloud Music
- KuGou Music (In Development)

### Low-Precision Matching

- Apple Music
- Spotify
- KuWo Music

## Troubleshooting Music Recognition

Remember the **Getting Started** content? Let's walk through it:

1. Enter the **maximum expanded state**
2. Switch to the **last page** of the **bottom bar** (the last **small dot**)
3. Click **Song Settings** in the **left sidebar**
4. Check if your music app is in the player whitelist; if not, add it below
5. Scroll through the **three dots** in the **right sidebar** to switch pages and check relevant settings
